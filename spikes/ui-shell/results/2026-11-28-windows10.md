## Observations

- egui keeps redrawing while idle and while minimized (55–158 frames per
  run, varying between runs) where Slint and iced stop. PRODUCT R4 asks hidden
  or minimized UI to avoid unnecessary redraw. This may be the shell's own
  repaint scheduling rather than egui itself; not investigated yet.
- iced holds about 70 MiB more working set and private memory than the other
  two in every scenario.
- Slint and egui are close on idle memory. Slint grows by about 18 MiB while
  speaking indicators animate.
- Window appearance is 30–60 ms for all three after the first run.
- Most private memory is likely the GPU driver (OpenGL on this AMD card);
  not separated here.
